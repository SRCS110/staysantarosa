Artistry in Wood 2026: Fine Woodwork Downtown

The Museum of Sonoma County's 37th annual woodworking show runs November 21 to January 31, two blocks from Art House and a short walk from Courthouse Square.

  2. Blog

← All stories

Stay Santa Rosa · Nov 6, 2026

Every winter, the Museum of Sonoma County fills its galleries with some of the finest woodwork made in the West. The 37th Annual Artistry in Wood opens on Saturday, November 21, 2026, and runs through January 31, 2027, with new pieces from more than 30 local and national artists. It is one of the most respected juried woodwork shows in California, and it sits right in downtown Santa Rosa, so it is easy to fold into a weekend of dinners, tasting rooms and holiday lights.

What to expect

Artistry in Wood is presented by the Sonoma County Woodworkers Association in partnership with the museum. Member pieces are reviewed by a jury before they are accepted, and a separate panel of outside judges then awards prizes across categories that include furniture, turnery, boxes and artwork. Expect everything from heirloom cabinets and chairs to turned bowls, sculpture and marquetry, with the grain, joinery and finish close enough to study.

The top honors are the Awards of Excellence and Best in Show, and winning pieces from past years have gone on to appear in Fine Woodworking magazine. Visitors get a say too: after you have walked the galleries, you can vote for your favorite piece to be crowned the People's Choice.

Hours, admission and dates

  • Dates: November 21, 2026, to January 31, 2027.
  • Hours: Wednesday to Sunday, 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. The museum is closed Monday and Tuesday.
  • Admission: $12 adults, $10 seniors 62 and over, $6 students, free for children 13 and under. EBT cardholders get in free through Museums for All.
  • Address: 425 7th Street, Santa Rosa, in the heart of downtown.

Check museumsc.org before you go for holiday closures, gallery talks and free family days during the run.

Also on at the museum this winter

From December 12, Golden California: Landscape Masterworks Mountains to Coast brings California landscape painting from the 19th century onward, from the snowy Sierra to the rocky Pacific shore. Visit after mid-December and one ticket covers both shows, plus the ongoing Santa Rosa History gallery. If the weather turns, our rainy day in Santa Rosa guide pairs the museum with the Schulz Museum and Snoopy's Home Ice.

Make an afternoon of it downtown

The museum is an easy walk from everything else downtown, so you can leave the car parked:

  1. Lunch first: pick a spot from our downtown dining guide. Several of the restaurants on 4th Street are five minutes on foot.
  2. The galleries: give Artistry in Wood an unhurried hour or more, and save time to cast your People's Choice vote on the way out.
  3. A late-afternoon pour: Russian River Brewing Co. is on 4th Street, or browse the wineries and breweries list for a downtown tasting room.
  4. Dinner: late-night sushi and Korean barbecue are about a block from the museum on 7th Street. The Santa Rosa restaurant list has hours and walk times for the rest.

Visiting over the holidays? The Thanksgiving weekend guide covers what is open across town, and the things to do in Santa Rosa page has more for a longer stay.

Good to know

  • The museum is open Wednesday to Sunday only, so plan Monday and Tuesday around other stops.
  • It is closed on Thanksgiving Day (November 26), Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.
  • The show closes January 31, 2027. Come back more than once and you can see it before and after the People's Choice is decided.

Stay steps from the show

Art House Santa Rosa is two blocks up 7th Street from the museum, with its own rotating collection of work by Sonoma County artists and studios with full kitchens for a longer winter stay. Hotel E is a few minutes' walk away on Old Courthouse Square, in a 1906 landmark with the Enology Lounge downstairs.
