Where Art Lives

Art House Santa Rosa is a contemporary boutique hotel built around the belief that great design and authentic local culture belong together under one roof.

The hotel's rotating collection of works by Sonoma County artists transforms guest corridors, and common areas into living gallery space, curated to feel personal, not institutional. Every piece in the gallery is for sale, with proceeds supporting local artists directly.

Modern rooms feature clean architectural lines, modern art, and warm natural materials. The design resists the impulse to impose a single aesthetic. Instead, each space holds its own point of view, a quality the city itself has always embodied.

Located in the heart of downtown Santa Rosa, Art House puts guests steps from Downtown Santa Rosa's independent restaurants, weekly farmers market on 4th Street, and the Sonoma County Museum, with wine country beginning just out the door.

Frequently Asked Questions

What room types does Art House offer? +
Art House offers Standard Rooms with balconies, Studios with full kitchens and approximately 600 sq ft of space, and one-bedroom Apartment Suites with full kitchens and spacious layouts up to 800 sq ft.
What are the check-in and check-out times? +
Check-in is available from 4:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early arrivals and late check-outs may be accommodated based on availability. Contact the front desk at (707) 545-5400 for special requests.
Is Art House pet-friendly? +
Yes, Art House welcomes well-behaved pets. Please contact the hotel in advance to inquire about any applicable fees.
Is parking available? +
Art House has the only secured indoor parking facility in Santa Rosa. Availability is limited so guests are encoured to call and reserve in advance.
Do the studios and suites have full kitchens? +
Yes. Art House Studios include a fully equipped kitchen with modern appliances, ideal for extended stays or guests who prefer to cook. The Apartment Suites include a full kitchen, dining area, separate living room, and workspace.
Is Wi-Fi included? +
Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is included for all guests throughout the hotel and in every room, with no hidden fees or bandwidth limits.
How far is Art House from wine country? +
Art House is located in downtown Santa Rosa, the gateway to Sonoma wine country. Russian River Valley wineries are 20–25 minutes west, Dry Creek Valley is about 25 minutes north, and Sonoma Valley is 15 minutes away. The hotel concierge can arrange private tasting appointments.
How do I make a reservation? +
Book directly at arthousesantarosa.com for the best available rate with no OTA fees, or call the hotel at (707) 545-5400. The front desk is open 24/7.

Staying at Art House in downtown Santa Rosa

Art House sits in the walkable centre of Santa Rosa, which makes it a straightforward base for a Sonoma County trip. Old Courthouse Square, the 4th Street restaurants and the historic Railroad Square district are all within a short walk, and the Russian River Valley wineries begin about fifteen minutes west of the door.

The hotel is built around a rotating collection of work by artists from the region, so the building changes between visits. Studios and suites suit longer stays, and guests planning days out can use the Stay Santa Rosa guides: the restaurant directory, the winery and brewery directory and the attractions directory, all with current hours and directions.
