Getting here

Santa Rosa sits on Highway 101 in the middle of Sonoma County, north of San Francisco and inland from the coast. Charles M. Schulz Sonoma County Airport is a short drive north of downtown, and San Francisco and Oakland international airports are the usual alternatives. A car makes wine country far easier, though the downtown restaurants, tasting rooms and taprooms in our restaurant directory and winery and brewery directory are walkable from the downtown hotels.
