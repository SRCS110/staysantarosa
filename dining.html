Santa Rosa, California

About downtown dining

The restaurants in downtown Santa Rosa fit into a few square blocks, which is why so many visitors to Sonoma County end up staying in the city rather than driving out to it each evening. Old Courthouse Square sits at the center of it, and the kitchens fan out from there along 4th Street, Mendocino Avenue, B Street and into Railroad Square.

Density is the argument. Santa Rosa is the largest city in the county, so it carries a working restaurant scene rather than a handful of destination rooms, and most of it is close enough together that you can change your mind at the door and be somewhere else five minutes later. Wine country dinners usually involve a car, a route and a designated driver. Here you leave the car where it is, walk out, and eat.

01 · Where to start

Three rooms by the square

The square does most of the heavy lifting. These three sit within a few minutes of each other and cover three different kinds of evening.

French

Augie's French

French · Stark Reality Restaurants

The newest concept from James Beard-nominated restaurateurs Mark and Terri Stark, classic French bistro cooking in an intimate, polished room. Already earning regulars fast.

augiesfrench.com
Steakhouse

Stark's Steak & Seafood

Fine Dining · Oyster Bar · Sonoma Wine · 521 Adams St

The anchor of Santa Rosa's downtown fine dining scene, a proper steakhouse with a live oyster bar and a wine list that leans deep into Sonoma County producers. Consistently rated among the top fine dining experiences in the city.

starksrestaurants.com
★ #1 Yelp

Bird & The Bottle

Small Plates · American Fusion · 1055 4th St

Santa Rosa's most consistently top-rated restaurant, James Beard-nominated Mark and Terri Stark's modern tavern, blends Asian, Jewish, Southern, and East Coast flavors into irresistible small plates. The wood-fired grill, craft cocktails, and daily happy hour make it the city's busiest room.

birdandthebottle.com
02 · Beyond the square

The range widens

Push a block or two out and the menu changes completely: Greek and Mediterranean grilling on B Street, Italian on Mendocino Avenue, wood-fired Neapolitan pizza, Indian cooking with regional depth and one of the city's best-loved brunches.

Italian

Loro di Napoli

Neapolitan Pizza · Italian · Downtown

Authentic Neapolitan pizza from a wood-fired oven, blistered crusts, simple toppings, and the kind of restraint that lets the ingredients do the talking. Consistently one of Santa Rosa's favorites.

lorodinapolica.com
Greek

Kalimera Bar & Grill

Greek · Mediterranean · Downtown

A hidden gem for Turkish and Mediterranean food in Santa Rosa, grilled meats, mezze, and a bright bar program. Consistently tops Yelp's most-rated downtown lists.

kalimerabarandgrill.com
Italian

Eddie's Kitchen

Italian · Casual Fine · Downtown

A downtown gem from a chef who has cooked at some of Santa Rosa's best tables, tuna tartare, tuna meatballs, and Italian-leaning plates with a warm, welcoming energy. Reservations fill fast.

eddieskitchen.org
Indian

Darbar Kitchen

Indian · Downtown Santa Rosa

Traditional Indian cooking with regional depth, aromatic curries, tandoor-fired breads, and a menu that goes well beyond the expected. A local favorite for a reason.

darbarkitchensr.com
Farm-to-Table

The Spinster Sisters

California Cuisine · Brunch & Dinner · LGBTQ+ Landmark

One of Santa Rosa's most beloved restaurants, imaginative California farm-to-table cooking with a genuinely warm room. The brunch is legendary and the dinner menu earns every star in its Yelp ranking.

thespinstersisters.com
American

Beer Baron Kitchen

American · Craft Beer · Downtown

A downtown kitchen that takes its food as seriously as its beer list, thoughtful American fare and a well-curated tap selection in a comfortable neighborhood setting.

beerbaronsr.com
03 · After dark

Late night & wine bars

Downtown does not shut down the moment dinner service winds up. Ausiello's 5th Street Bar & Grill is the neighborhood answer after a concert, HanBul Korean BBQ and Haku Sushi sit close together on 7th Street, and these rooms keep the bar side of the evening going.

Taproom

Belly Left Coast

American · Taproom · Downtown

A Left Coast kitchen and taproom with an approachable, satisfying menu, good food, good beer, and the easy comfort of a place that knows what it is.

bellyleftcoastkitchenandtaproom.com
Lounge

J&A Lounge

Cocktail Lounge · Late Night · Downtown

A downtown cocktail lounge with a relaxed late-night vibe, live music, creative drinks, and the kind of room that gets better as the evening goes on.

jalounge.com
Wine Bar

Willi's Wine Bar

Small Plates · Global Wine List · Old Redwood Hwy

A Santa Rosa institution, eclectic small plates, an expansive wine list that rewards exploration, and the comfortable noise of a room that's been full for over twenty years. A Stark Reality Restaurants cornerstone.

williswinebar.net
04 · Plan your week

A practical way to plan the week

Book the one dinner that matters before you arrive, and leave the rest open. Tables at the busiest downtown rooms go first on Friday and Saturday evenings, but midweek the walkable core absorbs walk-ins comfortably.

Sonoma County grows a great deal of what its restaurants cook, so menus shift with what is available, and harvest, late summer into fall, is the busiest stretch at the tables as well as in the vineyards. Spend the day out among the wineries near Santa Rosa, then eat back in town.

Good to know

Common questions about eating here

Quick answers for planning meals in downtown Santa Rosa.

Where should I eat in downtown Santa Rosa?

Start on 4th Street and around Old Courthouse Square, where Augie's French, Loro di Napoli and Bird & The Bottle sit close together, with Stark's Steak & Seafood just off the square on Adams Street. Beer Baron Bar & Kitchen is a few doors along 4th Street. Every restaurant we cover is listed on this page.

What restaurants are walking distance from Old Courthouse Square?

Most of the downtown scene. Augie's French, Loro di Napoli, Bird & The Bottle and Belly Left Coast Kitchen are on 4th Street, Stark's is on Adams Street, Kalimera Bar & Grill is on B Street, and Eddie's Kitchen and El Coqui are on Mendocino Avenue. Railroad Square sits a short walk further west.

Do I need reservations for restaurants in Santa Rosa?

For weekend dinners at the busier downtown rooms, yes, and more so during harvest, which runs late summer into fall. Midweek you can usually walk in. Book the one dinner that matters and leave the others flexible.

Where do locals eat in Santa Rosa?

Away from the obvious. El Coqui and Eddie's Kitchen on Mendocino Avenue, Kalimera on B Street, HanBul Korean BBQ and Haku Sushi on 7th Street, and Ausiello's for a late one. The dining guide article goes into more detail on this.

What are the best places to eat in Santa Rosa?

For a first visit, stay downtown. Augie's French, Bird & The Bottle and Loro di Napoli on 4th Street and Stark's Steak & Seafood on Adams Street cover the classic dinner, while El Coqui, Kalimera and HanBul Korean BBQ are where locals go for something more casual. The local food guide covers brunch, Railroad Square and late-night options in more depth.

Are there restaurants open late in Santa Rosa?

Yes. Ausiello's 5th Street Bar & Grill, Belly Left Coast Kitchen on 4th Street, and HanBul Korean BBQ and Haku Sushi on 7th Street all run later than a standard dinner service. Hours vary by night and by season, so check the restaurant map before you set out.
