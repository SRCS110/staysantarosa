Questions about Santa Rosa hotel deals

Where do I get the best rate at Hotel E and Art House?

Book directly on the hotels' own websites. Hotel E guarantees its best available rate on direct bookings with no third-party fees, and the Black Friday code and the Art House Member Rate only work on the hotels' own booking pages.

Is there a Black Friday hotel deal in Santa Rosa?

Yes. Hotel E and Art House Santa Rosa both take 40% off direct bookings made between November 9 and December 5, 2026 with code BLACK26, for stays from November 9, 2026 through October 31, 2027. Subject to availability, blackout dates may apply, and it cannot be combined with other offers. State and local taxes apply.

What is the Art House Member Rate?

A free membership you can join at checkout that takes 17% to 22% off every night. It is Art House's only year-round discount and also unlocks offers reserved for past guests.

Is there a last-minute hotel deal in downtown Santa Rosa?

Yes. Both Hotel E and Art House Santa Rosa offer a Last Minute Booking Deal for reservations made no more than three days before arrival, for a limited time and subject to availability.

How can a local business list an offer on Stay Santa Rosa?

Email hello@staysantarosa.com with the offer, terms, dates and a link. Listings are free for Santa Rosa and Sonoma County businesses.
