Santa Rosa & Sonoma County

Sonoma Wineries

Santa Rosa sits inside four of Sonoma County's most celebrated AVAs, Sonoma Valley, Russian River Valley, Dry Creek Valley, and Alexander Valley, with world-class tasting rooms a short drive from downtown Santa Rosa. From cave tours to cult Pinot Noir, here's where to taste.

Sonoma Valley
Matanzas Creek Winery
Sonoma Valley · Bennett Valley AVA

Rustic country charm meets refined quality in Bennett Valley, Chardonnay, Merlot, Sauvignon Blanc, and Pinot Noir from a stunning estate known for its lavender fields and sweeping valley views.

matanzascreek.com →
St. Francis Winery
Sonoma Valley · Kenwood

Voted best tasting room in the region, award-winning wines paired with chef-crafted bites in a beautiful Sonoma Valley setting. Known for rich reds and a genuinely warm hospitality experience.

stfranciswinery.com →
Kunde Winery
Sonoma Valley · Kenwood · 1,850 acres

A fifth-generation family estate with 1,850 acres of vineyards stretching from valley floor to mountain ridge. Cave tours, cave tastings, and one of Sonoma Valley's most impressive estate experiences.

kunde.com →
Nearby Favorites
Balletto Vineyards
Russian River Valley · Santa Rosa

A family farm turned world-class winery, cool-climate Pinot Noir and Chardonnay from the heart of the Russian River Valley, with a relaxed tasting room and genuinely welcoming staff.

ballettovineyards.com →
Benovia Winery
Russian River Valley · Santa Rosa

Small-production Pinot Noir and Chardonnay from some of the Russian River Valley's most celebrated vineyards, precise, cellar-worthy wines from a winery that earns serious attention.

benoviawinery.com →
Paradise Ridge Winery
Russian River Valley · Santa Rosa

Hilltop views, sculpture gardens, and estate wines. Paradise Ridge combines art, nature, and Sonoma winemaking in one of the area's most scenically distinctive settings.

paradiseridge.com →
Dry Creek Valley
Dry Creek Vineyard
Dry Creek Valley · Healdsburg

The benchmark for Dry Creek Zinfandel, a pioneering estate that helped define the appellation. The picnic grounds and relaxed tasting room make it one of the valley's most pleasant afternoons.

drycreekvineyard.com →
Wilson Artisan Wines
Dry Creek Valley · Healdsburg

Family-owned and focused on old-vine Zinfandel, Petite Sirah, and Rhône varieties from the Dry Creek and Alexander valleys, bold, food-friendly wines with a personal touch.

wilsonartisanwines.com →
Our Staff Favorites
Larson Family Winery
Sonoma Valley · Carneros

A fun, family-friendly estate with a petting zoo, bocce courts, and seriously good Zinfandel and Merlot, the kind of relaxed afternoon winery that earns its place on any Sonoma itinerary.

larsonfamilywinery.com →
Breathless Wines
Russian River Valley · Healdsburg

Sparkling wines made in the traditional method from Russian River Valley fruit, elegant, celebratory, and worth seeking out. A distinctive addition to any Sonoma wine itinerary.

breathlesswines.com →
Ridge Winery
Dry Creek Valley · Lytton Springs

Old-vine Zinfandel-based blends with the depth and track record that earns a place in serious cellars worldwide. The Lytton Springs estate is Ridge's Dry Creek anchor. Tasting here is a rare experience.

ridgewine.com →
Bricoleur Vineyards
Russian River Valley · Windsor

A modern estate winery combining farm-fresh hospitality with cool-climate Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. On-site farm-to-table dining and a beautiful setting make it a full-day destination.

bricoleurvineyards.com →
Merriam Vineyards
Russian River Valley · Healdsburg

A boutique family winery known for elegant, estate-grown Pinot Noir and an elevated tasting experience, intimate, unhurried, and focused on wine that speaks for itself.

merriamvineyards.com →
DeLoach Vineyards
Russian River Valley · Santa Rosa

Certified biodynamic and a pioneer in Sonoma sustainability, DeLoach produces expressive Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and Zinfandel from organically farmed estate vineyards in the Russian River Valley.

deloachvineyards.com →
Santa Rosa & Sonoma County

Craft Breweries

Santa Rosa has been dubbed the Microbrew Capital of the U.S., with Russian River leading a scene that spans barrel-aged sours, hop-forward IPAs, and Belgian-style ales. All locations are within a short drive of downtown Santa Rosa.

★ Must Visit
Russian River Brewing Co.
725 4th St, Santa Rosa · 5 min walk from Hotel E

The brewery that put Sonoma on the global craft beer map. World-famous for Pliny the Elder (double IPA) and the cult annual release of Pliny the Younger (triple IPA, March 20–April 2). Year-round pours include Belgian ales, barrel-aged sours, and California IPAs. Second location with brewery tours in Windsor.

Signatures: Pliny the Elder · Blind Pig IPA · Supplication · STS Pils
russianriverbrewing.com →
Downtown
Cooperage Brewing Co.
Multiple Santa Rosa locations · 8 min walk

A downtown Santa Rosa staple with an energetic taproom and a rotating lineup of hazy IPAs, sours, and creative small-batch brews. Pinball machines, a pool table, and rotating food trucks round out the experience.

Signatures: Keg Slayer IPA · Bow Down · rotating sours
cooperagebrewing.com →
CA-Grown Hops
Fogbelt Brewing Co.
1305 Cleveland Ave, Santa Rosa · 10 min drive

Named for the coastal fog that nourishes Sonoma's redwoods, Fogbelt uses exclusively California-grown hops. The urban-hip taphouse pours reliable blonde ales, IPAs, and stouts alongside innovative seasonals.

Signatures: Atlas Blonde · Del Norte IPA · Armstrong Stout
fogbeltbrewing.com →
Barrel-Aged
Shady Oak Barrel House
420 1st St, Santa Rosa · 8 min walk

Sonoma County's home for wild ales and barrel-aged sours, complex, tart, and endlessly rotating. A cool casual taproom with the kind of experimental program that draws serious beer travelers from across the state.

Signatures: Wild ales · Barrel-aged sours · Rotating experimental
shadyoakbarrelhouse.com →
Since 1992
Moonlight Brewing Co.
3350 Coffey Ln, Santa Rosa · 12 min drive

A Santa Rosa institution since 1992, small, hands-on, and fiercely independent. Moonlight brews traditional lagers and ales with artisan precision. Death & Taxes and Reality Czeck are among the county's most beloved year-round pours.

Signatures: Death & Taxes · Reality Czeck · Bombay By Boat IPA
moonlightbrewing.com →
Sebastopol
Crooked Goat Brewing
The Barlow, Sebastopol · 20 min drive

A passion project turned People's Choice Award winner at The Barlow in Sebastopol. Innovative IPAs and rotating seasonals in a family-friendly setting, and not available in stores, so the taproom is the only way to taste it.

Signatures: Hazy Hazy Hazy IPA · Coffee & Cream Ale
crookedgoatbrewing.com →
New · Nov 2025
Fieldwork Brewing Co.
Montgomery Village, Santa Rosa · 12 min drive

Berkeley's acclaimed small-batch brewery opened its Santa Rosa taproom in November 2025, bringing an ever-changing lineup of fresh hazy IPAs alongside Neapolitan and Detroit-style pizza. Spacious indoor and outdoor seating.

Signatures: Rotating hazy IPAs · fresh small-batch releases
fieldworkbrewing.com →

Tasting from a Santa Rosa base

Santa Rosa sits at the meeting point of four of Sonoma County's celebrated growing regions. The Russian River Valley wraps around the city to the west and is the reason so many Pinot Noir and Chardonnay producers are within fifteen minutes of downtown. Sonoma Valley runs southeast toward Kenwood and Glen Ellen. Dry Creek Valley and Alexander Valley sit north around Healdsburg, where the focus turns to Zinfandel, Cabernet Sauvignon and old vines.

Because the appellations fan out in different directions, it is worth choosing one per day rather than crossing the county twice. A morning in Russian River, lunch back in town and an afternoon in Sonoma Valley is a comfortable day. Healdsburg deserves its own. If you would rather not drive at all, several tasting rooms are inside Santa Rosa itself, and the Enology lounge at Hotel E pours Sonoma County by the glass on the ground floor.

Beer matters here too. Santa Rosa has been called the microbrew capital of the country, and the taprooms range from a globally known brewpub on 4th Street to barrel-aged and wild ale houses. The winery and brewery directory lists every one of them with current hours, and our wine country guide explains what each direction does best.
