Holiday Skating in Santa Rosa 2026: Snoopy's Home Ice and Winter Lights

Free outdoor ice shows, an Olympian on the ice and a holiday show starring Snoopy, all at the rink Charles Schulz built.

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Holiday Skating in Santa Rosa 2026: Snoopy's Home Ice
← All stories

Stay Santa Rosa · Nov 25, 2026

Santa Rosa has a better claim to holiday skating than most towns its size. Charles M. Schulz, the creator of Peanuts, built Snoopy's Home Ice in 1969, and the rink, which describes itself as having been called the world's most beautiful ice arena, still puts on a full December program: free outdoor shows, a professional ice show, sessions for toddlers and a figure skating club show starring Snoopy. Here is the 2026 schedule as the rink and its partners have published it, plus what we know so far about skating downtown.

Merry & Bright: free outdoor shows, December 4 to 6

Presented by Sonoma Clean Power and produced by Redwood Ice Theatre Company, Merry & Bright is a run of short outdoor skating shows in the courtyard at Snoopy's Home Ice. The shows are free and open to everyone, and no tickets are needed. The rink suggests arriving early to grab a hot cocoa and a good spot to watch.

  • Friday, December 4: 5 pm and 6 pm.
  • Saturday, December 5: 2, 3, 5 and 6 pm.
  • Sunday, December 6: 1, 2 and 3 pm.

One older Merry & Bright page on the rink's site still shows last year's times, so go by the 2026 holiday events page.

A Very Merry Ice Show, December 11 and 12

The following weekend, Redwood Ice Theatre Company's professional show moves indoors, with performances on Friday, December 11 at 8 pm and Saturday, December 12 at 6 pm and 8 pm. The cast features Olympian Polina Edmunds and two-time World Team members Kim Navarro and Brent Bommentre. Tickets are required. When we checked, the rink listed tickets as coming soon and no price had been posted, so look again before you plan an evening around it.

For small skaters and new skaters

Toddlers on Ice: Winter Trail on Wednesday, December 16 has two sessions, at 9:30 am and 11 am, for children under six with a grown-up. It costs $25 for a toddler and guardian, including skate or shoe grip-on rental, plus $10 for each additional child and $5 for each additional adult, and Peanuts characters make a guest appearance. Reservations are required and space is limited. The Thanksgiving edition on November 25 sold out.

If someone in your group wants to learn, the Snoopy Skating School runs single group lessons on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 3:45 to 4:15 pm on December 1, 3, 8, 10, 15 and 17. Each visit is $25 and includes the lesson, skate rental, admission to the 4:15 pm public session and a Learn To Skate USA membership. The school's holiday clinic ends with a free public performance on Thursday, December 17, from 5:30 to 6 pm.

'Tis the Season Starring Snoopy, December 28

Between Christmas and New Year's, the Santa Rosa Figure Skating Club puts on its holiday show on Monday, December 28 at 4:30 pm and 6 pm, directed by Karen Kresge, with Snoopy as the guest star who helps ring in the new year. Tickets are required and are sold through the link on the rink's homepage. No price was posted when we checked. One line on the rink's events page still reads 2025, but the homepage and current listings give December 28 for this season.

Public skating and the Warm Puppy Cafe

Public skating runs through November and December. Session times shift around school holidays and shows, so check the rink's online schedule on the day rather than relying on a fixed timetable. Inside, the Warm Puppy Cafe serves sandwiches, salads, soups, fries and a full hot cocoa menu, with a view over the ice. The Charles M. Schulz Museum is just down the street, and the rink suggests making a day of the Schulz campus, which also takes in the Children's Museum.

Winter Lights on Old Courthouse Square

Downtown, the Santa Rosa Metro Chamber's Winter Lights season opens with the free Tree Lighting Celebration on Friday, November 27, from 4 to 7 pm on Old Courthouse Square. Expect live entertainment, holiday drinks and treats, photos with Santa and the lighting of the Courthouse Square tree.

In recent seasons the Chamber has also set up a synthetic skating rink on the square that uses no water or electricity. In 2024 a ticket was $11 for a 45-minute session, with skate rental included. When we checked this week, the 2026 rink dates, hours and prices had not been confirmed, so look at downtownsantarosa.org/winterlights before you go.

Good to know

  • Snoopy's Home Ice: 1667 West Steele Lane, Santa Rosa, (707) 546-7147.
  • Nov 27: Winter Lights Tree Lighting, Old Courthouse Square, 4 to 7 pm, free.
  • Dec 4 to 6: Merry & Bright outdoor shows, free, no tickets.
  • Dec 11 to 12: A Very Merry Ice Show, tickets required.
  • Dec 16: Toddlers on Ice, $25 toddler and guardian, reserve ahead.
  • Dec 17: Snoopy Skating School free performance, 5:30 pm.
  • Dec 28: 'Tis the Season Starring Snoopy, 4:30 and 6 pm, tickets required.

Stay downtown for the holidays

Hotel E is in a historic building right on Old Courthouse Square, so the Winter Lights tree is outside the door. It has continental breakfast in the morning, an evening happy hour after a day out and valet parking. Art House Santa Rosa is also downtown, at 620 7th Street. Snoopy's Home Ice is on the north side of town, an easy drive for an afternoon show. If the weather turns, our guide to indoor things to do in Santa Rosa has more ideas.
